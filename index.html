Why offline

Hosted AI SRE is a non-starter where data can't leave.

Incident data is some of the most sensitive data you have: logs, configs, service maps and secrets in stack traces. The teams with the most to lose from an outage are the ones who can't hand it to a cloud AI tool.

Data residency

Sovereignty rules keep data inside the country, and often inside your own tenancy. A tool that ships telemetry to a vendor's cloud fails the review.

No route out

Air-gapped networks have no internet at all. SaaS agents can't install, can't reach their model, and can't be updated online.

No standing vendor access

Security teams won't give an outside service standing access to production. Investigation and fixes have to run under your own controls.

$80B

Gartner expects worldwide sovereign cloud infrastructure (IaaS) spending to reach about $80 billion in 2026, mostly from governments. Every one of those environments needs operating, and many of them can't send that work to a hosted AI.

Source: Gartner press release, February 2026.

How it works

From alert to root cause to an approved fix.

KubeSREAI does the first hour of an incident for you, from the moment something breaks to a fix you can approve.

Detect

Watches your clusters and alerts continuously, and opens an investigation the moment something fails.

Investigate

Pulls the metrics, logs and traces around the incident and follows the dependency chain to separate the cause from the symptoms.

Explain

A fine-tuned local model writes the root cause in plain language, citing your own runbooks and past incidents first.

Fix, with approval

Proposes a fix from a list of approved actions, applies it only under your policy, verifies the result, and escalates to a person when unsure.

You choose how much it does: Diagnose onlyApprove each actionAuto, within policy

Security & procurement

Built for your security review.

What your security and procurement teams will ask for is part of the product, not an afterthought.

One offline bundle

Every image pinned by digest, the model, an SBOM, a vulnerability scan and checksums, plus a script to mirror it into your own registry. SBOM available on request.

No phone-home

No external model API and no vendor telemetry. Optional outbound features are off by default, so an air-gapped install sends nothing.

Human in the loop

Installs in diagnose-only mode. Every action is checked against your policy, and an approved action can't run twice.
