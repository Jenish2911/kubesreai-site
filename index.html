How it works

From incident to resolution — automatically.

1

Detect

K8sGPT operator scans cluster state continuously. Prometheus metrics and live pod logs feed a rolling anomaly baseline.

2

Root-cause

Fine-tuned Phi-3.5-mini + RAG queries (postmortems / runbooks / K8s docs, Tempo trace graph) distinguish root cause from symptom.

3

Propose

Remediation is proposed as concrete kubectl commands, matched to the failure mode — bump memory, roll back, or restart.

4

Approve & Apply

Autonomous fixes are off by default. When enabled, only allowlisted non-destructive actions run; escalations need human sign-off in the operator console or CLI.

Capabilities

Everything your SRE team needs. Nothing they don't.

Proactive Detection

Event-driven watcher triggers analysis before anyone opens a ticket. It reads K8s events and Prometheus alerts as they fire, and peer-relative z-scores rank each component against its own baseline — no static thresholds to hand-tune.

Root Cause, Not Symptoms

RAG plus a fine-tuned model localises the true root cause across the service graph. It separates the failing dependency from its downstream noise.

Self-Healing Remediation

Identifies the failure mode and applies the least-invasive fix — bump memory, roll back, restart — then verifies the workload stays healthy before resolving, or escalates to a human. Model-guided fix choice Coming soon: the model helps choose the next fix from a checked set; it never runs commands itself.

Safe by Default

Installs in diagnose-only mode with no cluster writes. Approve and auto modes are opt-in. Every action passes one validator, only in namespaces you allow, and pods pass the Kubernetes “restricted” security profile. Ack, resolve, or approve from the operator console or CLI.

One-Click Deploy

One Helm chart installs the model server (llama.cpp on CPU, or vLLM on GPU), the RCA API with its built-in knowledge store, and the watcher — wired to your existing Prometheus, K8sGPT, and Tempo. Installs offline in under 10 minutes on a prepared cluster.

Air-Gapped Delivery

One checksummed bundle with every image pinned by digest, the Helm chart, the model, an SBOM and a mirror script for any OCI registry (Harbor, Artifactory, Nexus and others). Tested with outbound traffic blocked.

Runs on CPU

A quantized model in about 4 GB of RAM, with no GPU needed. On CPU an answer takes minutes, which is fine for event-driven RCA; a GPU makes it faster.

Your Runbooks First

Load your runbooks and wiki pages as knowledge packs. They are cited ahead of ours, fully offline.

Audit & One Console

An audit log of every approval and action. One login, roles per cluster and namespace, and one console across clusters Coming soon.

Zero egress

Your data never leaves your cluster.

Not a policy. An architectural guarantee.

How

The fine-tuned model runs locally (llama.cpp on CPU or vLLM on GPU) — no external model APIs, and your logs, metrics, traces, and prompts stay in-cluster. Optional outbound notifications and model-artifact pulls are off by default, so air-gapped installs send nothing.

Why it matters

Regulated industries — finance, healthcare, government — cannot send production logs to external AI APIs. This is a hard procurement requirement, not a nice-to-have.
